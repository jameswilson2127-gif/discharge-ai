Privacy Policy

Last updated:

In short: we store your account (your Google e-mail), your plan, your saved templates and counts of how often you use the AI. We do not store patient photographs, the information read from them, or the summaries you create. A patient name, if you type one, never leaves your device.

1. Who is responsible

is operated by . We are the Data Fiduciary for your account information. For patient information you submit, you or your Institution are the Data Fiduciary and we act as your Data Processor (see our Terms of Use). This policy follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

2. What happens to patient information

  1. You take or choose photos in your browser. Photos are re-encoded on your device, which removes embedded metadata such as location. You must black out identifiers, or confirm a page shows none, before a photo can be sent.
  2. When you tap "Read photos", the photos are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server function, which passes them to Google's Gemini API to read the text.
  3. The extracted information is returned to your browser. Our server also removes identifier-like numbers (for example Aadhaar, ABHA and phone numbers) as an extra safeguard. Nothing from the photos or the result is written to our database or logs. The photos exist only in the memory of that request.
  4. You review the information on your device. When you tap "Write discharge summary", only the reviewed clinical information is sent the same way to write the draft text. The patient name and the dates you typed are not sent.
  5. The draft appears in your browser for you to edit, print or copy. We do not keep a copy.

We use Google's paid Gemini API service. Under Google's terms for paid services, Google does not use the content to improve its products; Google may keep it briefly to detect abuse, as described in its Gemini API terms.

3. Information stored on your device

  • The case you are working on (patient name and dates if you typed them, the reviewed information and the draft) is kept in this browser tab's session storage so a reload doesn't lose your work. It is deleted when you close the tab, sign out, press "New patient", clear it after printing, or automatically after minutes. Photos are kept in memory only and are lost on reload.
  • Your sign-in token and last-used template are kept in local storage so you stay signed in.

On a shared computer, always sign out when you finish.

6. Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Database access is restricted so each user can read only their own data, and administrative keys are kept only on the server. Patient content is not stored, so it cannot be exposed in a breach of our database. If a personal-data breach affecting you does occur, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account information, templates and terms acceptances: until you delete your account.
  • Usage and payment records: kept after account deletion with the link to you removed, for accounting and as required by tax law.

8. Your rights

  • Access: download everything we store about you from the account menu → "Download my data".
  • Correction: edit your templates in the app, or write to us.
  • Erasure: delete your account from the account menu → "Delete account". This cancels any subscription and erases your account, templates and terms history.
  • Nomination: you may nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to; write to us.
  • Grievances: write to . We aim to reply within 7 days and in any case within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

The Service is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, we will ask you to review and accept it again before you continue using the AI features.
