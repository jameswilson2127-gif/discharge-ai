Built so patient data doesn't stick around

The app is designed to hold as little patient information as possible, and nothing for longer than it needs to.

  • Photos are never stored. They are read once by the AI and discarded. No copy is kept on our servers.
  • No IDs are collected. We never ask for UHID, IPD number, phone or address. Those lines print blank for you to fill by hand.
  • The name stays on your device. If you type it, it is used only for printing and is never sent anywhere.
  • You stay in charge. The AI writes a draft. Nothing prints until you confirm you have checked it.
